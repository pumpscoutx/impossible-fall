THE IMPOSSIBLE FALL

A scroll-driven WebGL artwork. A cliff face and a falling stream are viewed as if the world itself tips over. Progress through the piece is measured from 0 to 1.

Chapter 1 — The Impossible Fall (0.00–0.10)

The title runs vertically down the left cliff.

Chapter 2 — Water or Cloth (0.12–0.30)

A small question follows the waterfall line: was that water, or cloth?

Chapter 3 — Descend (0.30–0.48)

The letters of the word descend fall and separate as you go deeper.

Chapter 4 — Rotation (0.45–0.58)

The page and the world rotate together up to ninety degrees.

Chapter 5 — The Dark (0.55–0.66)

The camera enters the dark interior.

Chapter 6 — The One True Viewpoint (0.64–0.78)

You must find the one true viewpoint.

Chapter 7 — The Procession Listens (0.74–0.88)

The procession is listening. Clicking reveals short poetic lines about stone, water and cloth.

Chapter 8 — Everything Falls Apart (0.84–0.94)

The image disintegrates.

Chapter 9 — Return (0.94–1.00)

You have returned. Something has changed.

Controls

Scroll, drag, or use the arrow keys, W, S, J, K, space, page up and page down to move. Home and End jump to the start and end. Escape returns smoothly to the beginning. A small toggle switches between calm and full motion.
